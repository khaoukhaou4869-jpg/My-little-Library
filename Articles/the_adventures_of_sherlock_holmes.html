The book review

The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

A sharp-eyed detective, a loyal companion, and twelve ingenious cases on the streets of Victorian London.

Overall review

This collection is a delightful introduction to Sherlock Holmes: clever, briskly paced, and full of the pleasure of watching a mystery fall into place. Holmes and Watson's contrasting temperaments give the stories warmth as well as intrigue.

Review in detail

Each case offers a fresh puzzle, from curious clues to surprising motives. The short-story format makes the collection easy to dip into, while Watson's narration keeps Holmes's deductions engaging and human. The settings and small details give each mystery its own character.

Who should read it

Readers who enjoy classic mysteries, memorable detective duos, and satisfying solutions will feel right at home. It is also a welcoming starting point for anyone curious about Holmes.

If you liked this, try…

  • The Hound of the Baskervilles by Arthur Conan Doyle (a suspenseful mystery on a lonely moor)
  • The Sign of the Four by Arthur Conan Doyle (a fast-paced case involving a missing treasure)
  • The Memoirs of Sherlock Holmes by Arthur Conan Doyle (more clever cases with Holmes and Watson)
